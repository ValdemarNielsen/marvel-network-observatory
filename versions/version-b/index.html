    Protocol / caveat

    What this map is, and is not.

    01

    Roster before arrows

    Add all 303 characters first; then add the 1,784 directed links. That is how 17 isolates survive.

    02

    Direction has meaning

    A → B means A’s Wikipedia article links to B’s. It does not mean friendship, a fight, or popularity.

    03

    Collapse with care

    350 pairs link both ways. Collapsing them gives 1,434 pairs and mean undirected degree 9.47.
