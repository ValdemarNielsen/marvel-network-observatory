05 What we can honestly claim

Importance is a question, not a score.

Our answer

Spider-Man dominates every headline ranking, but the interesting result lives lower down: Turbo has only five links and much more betweenness than the degree sequence predicts. The network is resilient to a single removal, yet targeted attacks fragment it faster than random loss. Centrality therefore describes different kinds of importance, and the right choice depends on whether we care about popularity, reach, control, or prestige.

We can say

Turbo is an unexpectedly strong broker relative to degree-preserving null networks.

We cannot say

Turbo is the most important Marvel character in every meaningful sense.
