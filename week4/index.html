A move is not a conversion.

Community detection does not discover a philosopher's true school. It groups pages whose links are unusually dense. A move tells us that repeated links changed which neighborhood offered the stronger statistical fit.

Weights are not decoration. They change the question from “is there a link?” to “how much attention flows through it?”

02 · Shake the algorithm

A community map is a proposal, not a verdict.

Louvain makes randomized choices. We ran the weighted version twenty times, then compared every pair of partitions. Good maps recur, but they are not identical.

What surprised us

The network does not thin evenly.

At α = 0.05 the largest connected piece contains 130 philosophers. At α = 0.10 it jumps to 465. A small relaxation reconnects entire intellectual regions.

Our answer

Wikipedia's philosopher network has a stable large-scale shape and unstable borders. Weighting repeated links makes the division statistically sharper, but it moves nearly one in five philosophers. The right lesson is not that one partition is true. It is that every community map encodes a choice about what counts as evidence.
